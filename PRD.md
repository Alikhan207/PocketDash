# PocketDash — PS1 scope

Primary user: a college student running a small printed T-shirt and crochet gifts business, alone or with informal help.

Required complete workflows:
- Customers → itemised invoice draft → issue → advances/partial/final manual payments.
- Collections: who owes how much, due date, days overdue and last follow-up.
- Editable invoice-based reminder, copy and WhatsApp draft; explicit seller confirmation of sending.
- Upcoming supplier/material/delivery bills and paid-date tracking.
- Opening cash with start-of-day effective date; actual cash separated from receivables and planned spending.
- 30/60/90-day cash forecasts, unscheduled overdue collections, clear assumptions and shortage date/amount.
- A configurable 15-day collection delay compared with baseline, with reset that leaves records unchanged.
- Actionable overdue-invoice, upcoming-bill, missing-date and cash-shortage alerts.
- Persistence, seller isolation, fictional public demo, readable mobile-oriented UI, INR/Kolkata dates.

See README.md for data rules and REVIEW.md for completed validation and limitations. No marketplace, checkout, bank integrations, automatic messaging or AI prediction is in scope.
